# Photographic scene assets, 0.8.6

Generated for Nth Desktop 3D with the built-in image generation tool. The supplied user images guide composition and materials; stock screenshots are not shipped as textures.

The runtime creates moving glass panes and glasses, independent ice floes, and lava flow. The images supply photographic detail. The city atlas stores one sharp photograph beside a defocused copy of that same photograph, so optical views stay registered.

Texture processing: WebP quality 90; sky and ice 1024 × 1024, lava 1024 × 1536, city sharp/blur atlas 2048 × 1536. Every texture fits the GLES 2 minimum 2048 texture limit.

## Prompts

### glass_sky

Photorealistic architectural environment texture, square. Looking upward into a clean blue sky with detailed white cumulus clouds. Slim sharply detailed blue-glass office buildings frame only the extreme left/right and bottom corners; the central 80% is open blue sky and clouds. Natural daylight, real exposure and clear cobalt/cyan reflections. No freestanding panes, no center objects. This source image will reflect from live architectural glass geometry. No UI, text, logos or watermark.

### focus_city

Extremely realistic razor-sharp city photograph, portrait 2:3. Clean modern waterfront city at blue hour, finely detailed layered glass and stone office towers and warmly illuminated windows, subtle mountains behind. Sky fills upper quarter, river reflections lower quarter, interesting crisp building detail across center and both sides. Deep focus everywhere. Beautiful restrained natural evening lighting. No glasses or lenses; those are live geometry added by the app. No blur, bokeh, people, text, logos, watermark or UI.

### ice_material

Photographic material texture, square, directly overhead. Continuous thick translucent arctic blue ice: layered turquoise/cobalt depth, many trapped tiny white air bubbles at varying depths, fine hairline fractures, frost dust, glassy inclusions, a few irregular cloudy white patches. Extraordinary natural microdetail and wet specular glints. Edge-to-edge continuous ice, no horizon, no shoreline, no large separated floes or open-water gaps, no objects, text, logos or watermark. Live geometry will create separate floating pieces; this image supplies their realistic material.

### lava_material

Extremely realistic close aerial photograph of a winding active lava river in jagged black basalt, portrait 2:3, edge to edge with no horizon. Sinuous hot yellow-orange molten channel runs from top center to lower center, with thinner branching glowing cracks through rough dark raised volcanic plates. Visible real fluid striations, cooling black skin, red-amber edges, luminous centers, deep rugged basalt microtexture and warm reflected light beside the river. Basalt takes about 65% of frame. All sharply resolved, believable volcanic relief, no fantasy neon, people, buildings, text, logos, watermark or UI. Animation is added separately by app.
